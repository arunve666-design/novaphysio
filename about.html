About Nova Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation

Your journey to recovery starts here — at Chudala Junction, Taliparamba, Kannur.

Move. Heal. Rebuild. Live Better.

Nova Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation brings quality, personalised physiotherapy care to the Chudala community in Taliparamba, Kannur, Kerala. We combine hands-on manual therapy with modern rehabilitation techniques across orthopedic, neuro, sports, pediatric and stroke recovery.

Our goal is simple: help every patient recover safely, regain movement, and get back to the life they want to live.
